Hi Nancy

Below Sample workflow – Amspec Brazil

External customer:

  * Quotation Creation: The laboratory generates a Quotation detailing all the requested tests (Quotation need describe, LoQ for Analyte and TAT for Analysis).
  * Contract Review: The Laboratory Manager performs a critical review of the Quotation before it is sent to the customer. (we need a field in the Quotation with “review by”
  * Client Approval: The customer approves the Quotation and ships the sample to the laboratory. (Field in the Quotation for the customer to sign)
  * Sample Submission: The sample is ideally sent with the Quotation or with a clear indication of the tests to be performed linked to that Quotation. With Imara Lims the customer can fill Test Request Form (portal). But is very import this Request Form linked with a Quotation. We need discuss about Form next meeting.
  * Reception & Integrity Check: Laboratory reception receives the sample and inspects its transport conditions and physical integrity.
  * Technical Verification: If the initial check passes, the sample goes to the testing area, where the laboratory staff verifies all technical information.
  * Preparation & Testing: Once everything is confirmed correct, the analyst prepares the sample, executes the analyses, and enters the results into the system.
  * Specialist Review: A technical specialist reviews the entered data, results, and quality control parameters for approval.
  * Managerial Review: After the specialist's approval, the Laboratory Manager reviews the entire Test Report, cross-checking all information, results, and specifications.
  * Report Delivery: Once final approval is granted by the manager, the Test Report is officially sent to the customer.

Internal customer (Amspec Inspections)

  * Sample Submission: The sample is sent to laboratory with Amspec information, for Amspec Inspection we don´t need customer approved. With Imara Lims the Ampec Inspections can fill Test Request Form (portal). But is very import this Request Form linked with a Quotation.
  * Reception & Integrity Check: Laboratory reception receives the sample and inspects its transport conditions and physical integrity.
  * Technical Verification: If the initial check passes, the sample goes to the testing area, where the laboratory staff verifies all technical information.
  * Preparation & Testing: Once everything is confirmed correct, the analyst prepares the sample, executes the analyses, and enters the results into the system.
  * Specialist Review: A technical specialist reviews the entered data, results, and quality control parameters for approval.
  * Managerial Review: After the specialist's approval, the Laboratory Manager reviews the entire Test Report, cross-checking all information, results, and specifications.
  * Report Delivery: Once final approval is granted by the manager, the Test Report is officially sent to the customer.
