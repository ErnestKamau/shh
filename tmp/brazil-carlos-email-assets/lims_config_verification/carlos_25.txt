The Account Setting controls the customer's payment/credit terms:

  * Account Holder (OK): Approved credit account with no overdue balance. Work may proceed on credit, but a Purchase Order (PO) number is required.
  * Account Holder (Overdue): Credit customer with overdue invoices. This should be used temporarily when payment is overdue. Processing should be blocked until payment is received or an authorized approval is given.
  * Pay Upfront: No credit facility. This is intended for new, walk-in, cash, or higher-risk customers. An advance payment reference is required before sample reception and processing.

Practical rule:

  * Credit approved and account current → Account Holder (OK)
  * Credit suspended due to outstanding debt → Account Holder (Overdue)
