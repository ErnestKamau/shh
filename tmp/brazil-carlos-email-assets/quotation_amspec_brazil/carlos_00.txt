System Settings

Registered Companies – This section is intended for registering your company or companies (i.e., AmSpec entities). It is not used for registering client companies or suppliers.

* Suppliers are added through the Inventory module.
* Laboratories/Units that perform analyses are added under the Lab module and can then be assigned to users through the Personnel module.

CRM

Department – If you prefer, we can make this field optional. At the moment, it is configured as a mandatory field.

Customer Code

The Customer Code field in the spreadsheet template is optional. You may populate it if you already have internal customer codes that you would like to maintain; otherwise, it can be left blank.
