Hi Carlos,

Thank you for the questions. Here is how the Brazil quotation workflow maps to Imara.

Quotation in approval
Yes. This means the quotation is waiting for internal approval by the Lab Manager. It is not customer approval.

Quotation complete
This means the Lab Manager has approved the quotation internally. It does not mean the customer has approved.

After Complete, the next commercial steps are:
1. Send the quotation to the customer
2. Customer signs / Accept quotation (this is customer approval)
3. Record PO (or skip, if applicable)
4. Then the request is ready for sample reception

Sample in Lab reception with a quotation — next step

If the quotation is already Complete and the sample is at reception:

1. Samples Receiving→ fill the TRF (if not already done)
2. Process enquiry → Use existing quotation and link this quotation
3. Accept quotation (customer signature in Imara, even if they already signed on paper)
4. Record PO
5. Receive Samples (physical check-in)
6. Sample Integrity & Acceptance Check → Accept (this creates the lab job)

If the quotation was already sent and accepted before the sample arrived, skip to Record PO (if needed), then Receive Samples.

TRF first, then link the quotation
Yes, that is correct when the sample arrives with (or referring to) an existing quotation.

The other way, when the quotation is prepared before the sample arrives:

1. Prepare quotation → Lab Manager approval → Complete
2. From the quotation: Create enquiry from quotation (this generates the TRF from the quote)
3. Send to the customer
4. When the sample arrives: Accept quotation → Record PO → Receive Samples → Integrity Check

Your Brazil sequence in Imara is:

Customer request -> Prepare quotation (or capture TRF) -> Lab sends quotation -> After Lab Manager approval (Complete), send to customer ->
Customer signs -> Accept quotation (not Complete) -> Sample sent with quotation -> TRF + link quotation, or receive against the existing enquiry ->
 Lab receives sample -> Receive Samples → Integrity Check → Accept |

Please try one case this way and tell me if any step is unclear.
